Abhijit is a PhD student in Machine Learning at MBZUAI in Abu Dhabi, supervised by [Prof. Imran Razzak](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=GlXI4N8AAAAJ&hl=en) and [Prof. Nils Lukas](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=fmlEab4AAAAJ&hl=en), and co-founder and CTO of [MedOS Limited](https://aj-das-research.github.io/works/medos/). He also collaborates with [Prof. Pranav Rajpurkar](https://www.pranavrajpurkar.com/) at Harvard Medical School and [Prof. Zongwei Zhou](https://www.zongweiz.com/) at Johns Hopkins University. His research has three threads: grounding and reasoning in multimodal LLMs, so that a model's answers can be traced back to the evidence it was given; watermarking AI-generated content; and digital twins. Much of his published work applies these ideas to medical imaging. Before the PhD he was an AI Researcher at GE Healthcare India.
